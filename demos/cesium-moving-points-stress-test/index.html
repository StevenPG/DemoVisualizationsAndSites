Moving points, at scale

Ships, aircraft, ground vehicles and satellites, each a point primitive with its own heading and a leg to finish before it retires. Turn the counts up until it breaks. Click anything to promote it to a full entity.

Imagery and elevation are keyless ESRI services. The land/ocean mask is decoded at startup from the Natural Earth II textures inside Cesium's own bundled assets, which is what keeps ships in the water and vehicles on the ground.
