Marchward

Officers lead armies of thousands across sixty-nine kilometres of real ground. Supply is traced from your castle every turn. Surround theirs, starve it, then storm it.

The opposition

Map seed

The board is generated from this. The same seed and theatre always produce the same ground, so a map worth replaying can be written down.

Terrain, rivers and castle placement are generated per seed and drawn as extruded hex prisms over a bounded rectangle of the real world. Imagery is keyless ESRI; there is no Cesium Ion token and nothing is fetched from a third party at runtime.
